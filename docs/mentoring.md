# Mentoring and Review Gates

## Mentor / reviewer

**Raydo Matthee** — Skunkworks Academy

The IDR recommends a 30-day trial mentoring cadence because Fernanda prefers regular one-on-one feedback but has not yet confirmed a long-term mentoring arrangement.

## Fortnightly review agenda

1. Outcomes completed since the previous review
2. Evidence demonstrated
3. Quality, privacy and control issues
4. Blockers and support required
5. Decisions made
6. Actions, owners and due dates
7. Career-translation note

## Formal gates

| Gate | Agenda | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| First consultation | Validate roles, time, tools, language needs, existing evidence and project context. | Approve or revise placement and sprint. |
| 30 days | Baseline, positioning package, consistency and first artefacts. | Continue, narrow focus or adjust support. |
| 60 days | Process/data baseline, technical progress and blockers. | Approve prototype scope. |
| 90 days | Demonstrate workflow and dashboard; review evidence and career conversion. | Go, adjust or stop a learning track. |
| Quarterly | Business impact, credential relevance, leadership behaviour and role opportunities. | Set next-quarter outcomes. |

## Trial mentoring agreement

The first consultation should confirm confidentiality, expected preparation, feedback style, session frequency, cancellation arrangements, response times and how disagreements or unsafe project choices will be handled.
