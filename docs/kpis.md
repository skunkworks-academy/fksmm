# Development Goals and KPIs

## Development goals

| Goal | Measure / evidence | Target date |
|---|---|---|
| Career positioning | Target-role matrix, tailored CV, professional summary and ten-role opportunity list. | 31 Aug 2026 |
| Process and data baseline | Validated process map, data inventory and KPI dictionary. | 30 Sep 2026 |
| Automation prototype | Controlled low-code workflow with test cases and user guide. | 30 Nov 2026 |
| Decision dashboard | Executive dashboard with reconciled data and written commentary. | 31 Jan 2027 |
| Credential milestone | At least one assessed learning or certification milestone. | 31 Mar 2027 |
| Leadership pilot | Lead a small improvement initiative and present measurable results. | 30 Jun 2027 |
| Role transition | Stronger employment, expanded responsibility or approved transition plan. | 6 Aug 2027 |

## Operating KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Learning consistency | Structured sessions completed / planned sessions. | At least 85% monthly. |
| Evidence completion | Weeks with an accepted practical deliverable. | 10 of 12 sprint weeks. |
| Portfolio quality | Artefacts meeting problem, evidence, control, result and privacy criteria. | Minimum four accepted artefacts by March 2027. |
| Automation quality | Required test cases passed with documented exceptions. | 100% critical tests; no unresolved high-risk defect. |
| Business value | Improvement measured against an approved baseline. | One quantified pilot result by June 2027. |
| Career conversion | Targeted applications, interviews or internal progression discussions. | Monthly target set after positioning review. |
| Feedback response | Agreed review actions completed by due date. | At least 90%. |

## KPI evidence rules

- Every metric needs a definition, owner, source and review frequency.
- Business-value claims must state the baseline and calculation method.
- Estimated benefits must be labelled as estimates.
- Sensitive source data stays in approved private storage.
- Mentor sign-off is required for accepted milestone evidence.
