# Risks, Privacy and Governance

<div className="privacy-banner"><strong>Do not place confidential records in this public repository.</strong> Use synthetic or authorised data for labs, screenshots, dashboards and workflow examples.</div>

| Risk | Control | Early warning |
|---|---|---|
| Overcommitment or burnout | Limit baseline to 8–12 structured hours and agree sprint intensity. | Missed sessions, declining quality or unfinished work. |
| Too many tools and credentials | One analytics tool, one automation platform and one core credential at a time. | Multiple incomplete courses without evidence. |
| Experience not translated into market language | Connect every project to target-role competencies and interview stories. | Strong experience with weak interview conversion. |
| Confidential-data exposure | Synthetic data, least privilege, redaction and secure storage. | Personal, executive, financial or customer data in public evidence. |
| Automation without controls | Ownership, permissions, exceptions, testing, audit and rollback. | Unapproved workflow or unhandled failure path. |
| Credential-first behaviour | Require applied evidence for each credential. | Badges without demonstrated capability. |
| Mentoring mismatch | Trial period, explicit expectations and decision-focused reviews. | Sessions lack actions or psychological safety. |
| International transition uncertainty | Define target markets, work authorisation and role criteria early. | Broad applications with unclear fit. |

## Responsible-AI checklist

- Is the business purpose legitimate and documented?
- Is input data authorised and minimised?
- Are confidential details excluded from public AI tools?
- Are outputs verified against reliable sources?
- Is human review required before a consequential action?
- Are limitations and uncertainty clearly stated?
- Can the work be audited or reproduced?
- Is there a safe fallback when the AI output is unsuitable?

## Public evidence checklist

- [ ] Synthetic or expressly authorised data
- [ ] No contact, identity, payment or confidential business details
- [ ] Secrets and tokens excluded
- [ ] Screenshots sanitised
- [ ] Prototype or simulation clearly labelled
- [ ] Sources and assumptions documented
- [ ] Risks, limitations and rollback included
