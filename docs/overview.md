# Executive Summary

<div className="idr-badges"><span className="idr-badge">12-month roadmap</span><span className="idr-badge">30 / 60 / 90-day gates</span><span className="idr-badge">Instructor-led + applied labs</span><span className="idr-badge">Mentor: Raydo Matthee</span></div>

## Provisional placement

**Business Operations and Digital Transformation Practitioner**, with near-term positioning in AI-enabled executive support, process improvement, project coordination and operational analytics.

Fernanda enters this pathway with a strong base in executive and administrative support, stakeholder and calendar management, project coordination, process improvement, financial administration, supplier coordination, Microsoft Office, Google Workspace and AI productivity tools. The roadmap therefore builds from experience; it is not an entry-level administration programme.

## 12-month North Star

> By August 2027, Fernanda should be able to lead an AI-enabled business-operations improvement initiative, translate executive and operational needs into structured processes, produce decision-ready dashboards, configure a controlled workflow automation, and present a credible portfolio for senior executive-support, operations, project-coordination or business-transformation roles.

## Immediate outcomes

- Validate the target-role shortlist and professional narrative.
- Establish a data, spreadsheet and automation baseline.
- Select one safe operational process for the first applied project.
- Complete a 12-week instructor-led and evidence-based sprint.
- Build at least four accepted portfolio artefacts by March 2027.
- Lead one measured improvement pilot by June 2027.
- Secure a stronger role, expanded responsibility or approved transition plan by August 2027.

## Development principles

1. **Capability before credentials.** Every learning activity must produce evidence or improve a relevant process.
2. **Use synthetic or authorised data.** Public evidence must not expose executive, employee, customer or financial information.
3. **Build on prior experience.** Repeat basic content only when diagnostics identify a gap.
4. **Focus the technology stack.** Prioritise Microsoft 365, Power Platform and Google Workspace before adding unrelated tools.
5. **Translate learning into job language.** Each project must produce a CV bullet, interview story and portfolio artefact.
6. **Use sustainable intensity.** Maintain 8–12 structured hours weekly; use additional time only for agreed sprint periods.
