# 12-Week Applied Learning Sprint

<div className="idr-badges"><span className="idr-badge">Instructor-led</span><span className="idr-badge">8–12 hours weekly</span><span className="idr-badge">Evidence required</span><span className="idr-badge">Fortnightly mentor review</span></div>

| Week | Capability focus | Instructor-led session | Practical deliverable |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Career and capability baseline | Target-role analysis, strengths translation and learning governance. | Role matrix, baseline self-rating and professional summary. |
| 2 | Advanced spreadsheet analysis | Data structure, validation, lookup logic, pivot analysis and quality checks. | Analyse a synthetic operations dataset and document assumptions. |
| 3 | Process mapping and SOP design | Current/future-state mapping, roles, hand-offs and exceptions. | BPMN-style process map and two-page SOP. |
| 4 | Project coordination and agile execution | Charter, scope, RAID, work breakdown and meeting cadence. | Mini project plan, RAID register and stakeholder map. |
| 5 | AI productivity and prompting | Responsible AI use for executive and operations work. | Prompt library for briefings, correspondence, meeting analysis and process documentation. |
| 6 | Workflow automation fundamentals | Triggers, approvals, conditions, notifications, controls and rollback. | Prototype a controlled approval or task-coordination workflow. |
| 7 | Operational data visualisation | KPI design, Power BI concepts and executive storytelling. | Executive dashboard from synthetic data. |
| 8 | Finance and operations insight | Budget tracking, variance and service metrics. | Monthly operations and finance review pack. |
| 9 | Digital workplace optimisation | Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace collaboration and records governance. | Controlled team workspace and document-management standard. |
| 10 | Privacy, security and responsible AI | Classification, access, retention, vendor risk and acceptable AI use. | Privacy, access and responsible-AI control checklist. |
| 11 | Capstone build | Integrate process, data, automation and reporting. | Capstone prototype, test evidence and benefits estimate. |
| 12 | Executive presentation and career conversion | Present value, lessons, limitations and next actions. | Ten-minute presentation, portfolio pack, CV bullets and 90-day review. |

## Completion rule

A week is complete only when Fernanda can:

1. explain the concept;
2. demonstrate the output;
3. document decisions and limitations;
4. show that confidential information was protected; and
5. receive review feedback.

Watching content without producing evidence does not count as completion.
