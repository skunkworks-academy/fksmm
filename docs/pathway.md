# Recommended Development Pathway

## Primary track — AI-Enabled Business Operations

**Purpose:** Convert existing executive-support and operations experience into measurable digital-operating capability.

**Target role families:** Senior Executive Assistant, Executive Operations Coordinator, Project Coordinator, Business Operations Analyst and Operations Manager.

**Core capability set:** process mapping, operational analytics, AI-enabled productivity, workflow automation, stakeholder management and governance.

## Secondary track — Strategic Executive Support and Chief-of-Staff Readiness

**Purpose:** Develop strategic judgement, leadership cadence, executive communication and initiative governance.

**Target role families:** Executive Business Partner, associate Chief-of-Staff pathway, Programme Office Coordinator and Transformation Office Analyst.

**Core capability set:** executive briefings, decision logs, portfolio governance, meeting systems, business cases and change communication.

## Optional specialisation — Learning Operations and Practical Facilitation

Fernanda’s preference for instructor-led learning and practical lab creation can support a secondary capability in learning operations, training-programme coordination and business-skills facilitation.

## Professional positioning statement

> Fernanda is an experienced executive-support and business-operations professional who combines disciplined coordination, stakeholder management, financial administration and process improvement with modern AI productivity tools. She is developing applied capability in automation and analytics to help organisations operate with greater structure, speed and decision quality.

## Evidence-to-career conversion

Each completed deliverable must produce:

- a concise problem statement;
- a description of Fernanda’s role;
- a demonstration or screenshot using sanitised information;
- a measurable or testable result;
- a risk, limitation and governance statement;
- one CV achievement bullet;
- one structured interview story.
