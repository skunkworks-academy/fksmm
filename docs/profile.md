# Capability Profile and Priority Gaps

## Current strengths

| Capability | Provisional rating | Roadmap implication |
|---|---:|---|
| Executive support and administration | 4.8 / 5 | Use as the operational foundation for higher-value transformation work. |
| Stakeholder and communication management | 4.5 / 5 | Apply to executive briefings, facilitation and change communication. |
| Project coordination | 4.2 / 5 | Formalise scope, RAID, benefits and delivery governance. |
| Process improvement | 4.0 / 5 | Convert experience into documented process architecture and measurable outcomes. |
| Financial administration | 4.0 / 5 | Build operational-finance dashboards and variance commentary. |
| AI productivity tools | 4.0 / 5 | Add verification, privacy, prompt design and measurable productivity controls. |
| Strategic leadership | 3.3 / 5 | Strengthen decision framing, prioritisation and leadership cadence. |
| Data analysis and visualisation | 2.7 / 5 | Priority development area: clean data, KPI design and decision-ready reporting. |
| Low-code automation | 2.5 / 5 | Priority development area: controlled workflows, testing, exceptions and rollback. |

## Priority development gaps

| Priority | Development gap | Target evidence |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Advanced data analysis and visualisation | Decision-ready dashboard with documented KPI definitions. |
| 2 | Workflow automation and low-code design | Tested approval or coordination workflow with controls and rollback notes. |
| 3 | Process architecture and continuous improvement | Current/future process maps, SOPs and improvement backlog. |
| 4 | Strategic business management | Business case covering baseline, value, cost, risk and governance. |
| 5 | Technology governance and data protection | Data classification, access, retention and responsible-AI checklist. |
| 6 | Portfolio and market positioning | Targeted CV, professional narrative, portfolio and interview evidence. |

## Baseline validation tasks

- Complete a spreadsheet analysis exercise using synthetic operations data.
- Build or critique a basic dashboard and explain the KPI logic.
- Design a simple approval workflow and identify failure paths.
- Present a two-page process map and SOP.
- Produce a one-page executive decision brief.

Ratings are provisional until validated through practical exercises and mentor review.
